  - Overnight at Hotel. Meal: `(--/--/--)` or `(--/L/--)`
- **Day 02 (Intercity Transfer to Bangkok)**:
  - Breakfast at Hotel
  - 12.00pm Checkout from Hotel
  - Private Transfer Pattaya Hotel to Bangkok Hotel
  - 14.00 Check in at Bangkok Hotel or Similar 3*/4*
  - Chao Phraya Dinner Cruise (Join Transfer)
  - Overnight at Hotel. Meal: `(B/--/D)`

  - Breakfast at Hotel
  - Safari World & Marine Park with Lunch OR Dream World & Snow Town with Lunch (Join Transfer)
  - Overnight at Hotel. Meal: `(B/L/--)`

  - Breakfast at Hotel
  - Bangkok Temple and City Tour (Marble Temple, Golden Buddha) OR Sea Life + Madame Tussauds
  - Free For Leisure
  - Overnight at Hotel. Meal: `(B/--/--)`
- **Day 05 (Departure Day)**:
  - Breakfast at Hotel
  - 12.00pm Checkout from Hotel
  - Private Transfer Bangkok Hotel to Bangkok Airport
  - Tour End! Meal: `(B/--/--)`
